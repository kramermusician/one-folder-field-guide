# 10. Safety and privacy

Your folder will hold your life. Decide early what never leaves it.

## Rules worth writing on day one
- Drafts only. The AI never sends email, posts, publishes or deletes without your yes.
- Money, health, passwords, ID numbers: never in a file the AI shares, uploads or publishes. Better: keep them out of the folder.
- People files stay local. Never in a public repo, website or slide.
- Student work: think hard before an AI reads it. Check your school's policy.
- Nothing gets deleted outright. Move it to an archive or the Trash.

## Backups
- Dropbox keeps file history (restore a bad edit).
- Ask the AI to back up CLAUDE.md before big changes.
- Later: put your code (not your notes) in a private git repo.

## Before you publish anything
Ask the AI: "scan this for names, emails, addresses and private details." Then read it yourself.
