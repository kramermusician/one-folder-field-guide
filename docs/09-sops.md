# 09. SOPs (standard operating procedures)

An SOP is a checklist the AI reads before a type of job. When something goes wrong twice, it becomes an SOP line.

## Good first SOPs
- PDF_SOP: margins, fonts, tables that never overflow, a footer with your name, one image with its source.
- Slideshow_SOP: one idea per slide, big type, a question at the end.
- Voice_SOP: how you write. Five real sentences of yours, words you never use.
- Journal_SOP: after each work session, the AI writes what it built and why.
- Email_SOP: drafts only, your sign-off, how formal for whom.

## The failure journal

`past-work/failure-journal.md`: every time something breaks, one entry: what happened, why, the rule that now prevents it, and which file the rule lives in. Read it before big jobs.
