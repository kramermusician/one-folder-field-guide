# 11. Things to try (easiest first)

## Week 1
1. Record five memos a day. Look at what changed.
2. Mention three colleagues by name. Open their new people files.
3. Ask "brief me on <person>" before you see them.
4. Say "write that down as a rule" the first time the AI annoys you.
5. Open the folder in Obsidian, open Graph view, click around.
6. Ask "what should I work on today?"
7. End each session with /close-chat (journal, to-dos, rules saved).

## Week 2
8. Hire one teammate from the menu in 06.
9. Ask for a one-page handout. Correct it. Save the corrections to the PDF SOP.
10. Make a slideshow for a real class. Use the feedback box to leave notes per slide.
11. Write your Voice SOP: paste five things you wrote, ask it to describe your voice.
12. Add `courses/<course>.md` for each class with the schedule and goals.
13. Ask "who haven't I followed up with?"
14. Start the failure journal.

## Week 3 and on
15. Ask it to build a small web tool for class (a name picker, a timer, a vocab game).
16. Connect Gmail or Google Calendar for drafts only.
17. Have it write a weekly review of your to-dos every Friday.
18. Add a librarian: a catalog of every file you've made.
19. Photograph handwritten notes into the inbox; have them transcribed and filed.
20. Build a language tutor that knows your level.
21. Ask "what patterns do you see in my journal this month?"
22. Split CLAUDE.md: move project detail into project folders.
23. Make a maker-and-checker pair for something you publish.
24. Teach it your semester calendar and ask it to warn you 72 hours before a class with no materials.
25. Ask it to propose three new teammates based on what you've asked for this month.
26. Try a Shortcut that sends a photo plus a memo together.
27. Make a song chart or a setlist from a voice memo.
28. Run a "still want these?" cleanup of anything untouched for 30 days.
29. Have two AIs (Claude and Codex) do the same small job and compare.
30. Teach someone else. Explaining your system is the fastest way to improve it.
